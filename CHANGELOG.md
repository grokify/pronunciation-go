## [v0.1.0] - 2026-11-09

### Highlights

- `respell` turns IPA and dictionary respellings, as Wikipedia writes them, into respellings a plain-text speech engine reads as intended
- Several candidate respellings per word to play and choose from, since voices read unfamiliar words by their own rules

### Added

- `FromIPA` reads IPA into syllables with stress: stress marks and breaks start syllables, the longest legal English onset goes to the next syllable, affricates and diphthongs are joined, doubled consonants collapsed ([`d6684f3`](https://github.com/grokify/pronunciation-go/commit/d6684f3))
- `Word.Respelling` writes syllables in the style of Wikipedia's pronunciation respelling key, stressed syllables in capitals, as in LAM-bər-GHEE-nee ([`d6684f3`](https://github.com/grokify/pronunciation-go/commit/d6684f3))
- IPA in other languages (Italian, Spanish, French, German and more) brought to the nearest English sounds: pure vowels written long, nasal vowels with an n ([`8bbf330`](https://github.com/grokify/pronunciation-go/commit/8bbf330))
- `FromRespelling` reads a hyphenated respelling such as LAM-bər-GHEE-nee back into syllables ([`8bbf330`](https://github.com/grokify/pronunciation-go/commit/8bbf330))
- `Candidates` writes a word's sounds up to `MaxCandidates` ways: as one word, split where the stress falls, syllable by syllable, with simpler endings and with a consonant cluster split ([`116c97c`](https://github.com/grokify/pronunciation-go/commit/116c97c))
- `Suggest` reads text as Wikipedia writes it: English IPA between slashes, bracketed IPA in the language named before it (`Languages`), and hyphenated respellings, returning a `Suggestion` per word ([`116c97c`](https://github.com/grokify/pronunciation-go/commit/116c97c))
